# Status Report Instructions

Use `template.md` to prepare one report for each reporting week. Replace every bracketed prompt with current, verified information, then remove any unused placeholders.

## Fill In Each Section

- **Project, Owner, Week:** Use the project name, accountable owner or team, and the exact start and end dates in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
- **Executive Summary:** Lead with overall project health and the week's most important outcome or change. Keep it to one or two concise bullets and write for senior stakeholders.
- **Completed:** List work finished during this reporting week, including meaningful milestones. Do not list work that is still underway.
- **In Flight:** List active work and its next milestone or expected completion where known.
- **Risks / Blockers:** State the issue, its impact, and the mitigation or responsible owner. If there are none, write `None identified.`
- **Next Week:** List the main planned activities or priorities for the coming week.
- **Decisions Needed:** State the specific decision or approval, who needs to make it, and when it is needed. If none are needed, write `None.`

## Before Sharing

- Verify updates against Jira and team-lead inputs; do not guess when information is missing.
- Keep bullets brief, specific, and focused on delivery, impact, and action.
- Use consistent dates and distinguish completed work from work in progress.
- Remove all bracketed prompts and include `None identified.` or `None.` where appropriate instead of leaving sections blank.
- Exclude credentials and sensitive details that stakeholders do not need.
